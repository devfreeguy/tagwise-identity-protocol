Four things worth understanding before you build against TIP: how a tag is
normalized, how it becomes an address, what lives on-chain versus in the
mirror, and what the owner/wallet split actually means.

- [Tags](/docs/concepts/tags): the canonical form every tag is reduced to.
- [Addresses and PDAs](/docs/concepts/addresses-and-pdas): how a tag becomes an address.
- [Source of truth and the mirror](/docs/concepts/source-of-truth): what lives where, and who can write it.
- [Ownership](/docs/concepts/ownership): the owner controls the tag; the wallet is only where payments go.
