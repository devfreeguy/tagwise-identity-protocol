## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device when you visit.
They allow the site to remember information about your visit, such as your
preferences, and to function correctly across page loads.

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies Tagwise
Identity Protocol (referred to as "TIP", "we", "us", or "our") uses on
[tagwise.me](https://tagwise.me) and its subdomains, including
[docs.tagwise.me](https://docs.tagwise.me), and why.

## 3. Cookies We Use

We use only the categories of cookies described below. We do not use advertising
cookies, tracking cookies, or third-party behavioral profiling cookies.

### 3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the website to function. They cannot be disabled.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `__session` | Maintains your session state across page navigation | Session (deleted when you close your browser) |
| `csrf_token` | Protects against cross-site request forgery attacks | Session |

### 3.2 Preference Cookies

These cookies remember choices you make so we do not have to ask again.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `theme` | Remembers your light or dark mode preference | 1 year |
| `cookie_consent` | Records that you have acknowledged this cookie notice | 1 year |

### 3.3 Analytics Cookies

We use privacy-preserving analytics to understand how visitors use our site. No
personally identifiable information is collected, and no data is shared with
advertising networks.

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|----------|---------|----------|
| `_pk_id` | Self-hosted analytics | Distinguishes unique visitors using an anonymised identifier | 13 months |
| `_pk_ses` | Self-hosted analytics | Tracks a single session | 30 minutes |

If we add or change analytics providers, this table will be updated and the
"Last updated" date at the top of this page will change.

## 4. What We Do Not Use

We do not use:

- Advertising or retargeting cookies
- Social media tracking pixels
- Third-party behavioral profiling cookies
- Fingerprinting or device-tracking technologies

## 5. Local Storage and Session Storage

In addition to cookies, our documentation site
([docs.tagwise.me](https://docs.tagwise.me)) may use browser local storage to
remember your search history and sidebar position. This data never leaves your
device and is not transmitted to our servers.

## 6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

### Browser settings

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Instructions
for common browsers:

- [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac)
- [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09)

Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of this website.

### Opt-out of analytics

If you prefer not to be included in our analytics, you may opt out by enabling
the "Do Not Track" signal in your browser. We respect this signal and will
exclude your visits from analytics processing.

## 7. Cookies and Blockchain Data

Tagwise Identity Protocol resolves `@tags` to on-chain wallet addresses. This
resolution process does not use cookies. On-chain data, including wallet
addresses and tag registrations, is public by the nature of the Solana
blockchain and is not governed by this Cookie Policy. For information about
on-chain data, please read our
[Privacy Policy](/privacy).
